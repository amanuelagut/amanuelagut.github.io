Colombian Grain Price Dynamics During COVID-19: A Multi-Market Analysis

Angie Manuela Gutierrez-Oviedo · Fundación Universitaria del Área Andina · 2020
SIPSA-DANE FRED API USD/COP Brent Crude Multi-Product Analysis Correlation Matrix Multiple Regression COVID-19
Abstract
Resumen
This capstone integrates three parallel research briefs into a unified multi-market analysis of Colombian grain price dynamics during the COVID-19 pandemic (2018–2020). Using SIPSA-DANE wholesale price data across three products (fríjol, rice, corn) and two cities (Bogotá, Ibagué), combined with FRED macroeconomic series (USD/COP exchange rate, Brent crude oil), the analysis documents the synchronized "twin shock" that hit Colombian grain markets: Brent crude collapsed 76% (from $81.42 to $19.53/bbl) while USD/COP depreciated 43.7% (from COP 2,765 to COP 3,974/USD), with a correlation of r = −0.82. Domestic fríjol prices at Corabastos showed the highest volatility increase (CV rose from 6.02% to 11.34%, +88.4%), while rice remained stable. Regional price spreads between Bogotá and Ibagué widened during April–June 2020, indicating temporary market segmentation.
1.
Research Question
Pregunta de Investigación

What systematic price patterns emerge when crossing products, cities, macroeconomic variables, and time periods across Colombia's main grain wholesale markets during the COVID-19 shock?

The COVID-19 pandemic created a natural experiment in supply chain disruption. Colombia's centralized wholesale market structure — with Corabastos in Bogotá handling a disproportionate share of national grain flows — meant that lockdown-induced bottlenecks would manifest clearly in price data. This capstone tests whether the disruption was uniform or whether product-specific, geography-specific, and macro-level factors created divergent price trajectories.

3.
Methodology
Metodología

The capstone applies four analytical layers, integrating the methods developed across three research briefs.

Step 1 — Multi-dimensional descriptive statistics. Mean, standard deviation, coefficient of variation (CV), min/max computed for each product × city × period combination. The CV metric — introduced in RB1 — enables cross-product volatility comparison even when absolute price levels differ by an order of magnitude.

Step 2 — Correlation matrix. Pearson correlation coefficients computed across all series: three products (Bogotá prices), USD/COP, and Brent crude. The matrix reveals which variables move together under stress and which diverge. The strong negative correlation between USD/COP and Brent (r = −0.82) confirms Colombia's oil-export dependence.

Step 3 — Multiple regression. Using Excel's LINEST function, a model estimates: Fríjol_price = β₀ + β₁·USD/COP + β₂·Brent + β₃·COVID_dummy + ε. The COVID dummy (1 for Mar–Dec 2020, 0 otherwise) tests whether the pandemic effect is statistically distinguishable from macro movements. High collinearity (USD/COP vs. Brent r = −0.82) limits coefficient interpretability.

Step 4 — Base-100 multi-series chart. All series re-indexed to January 2018 = 100 (or March 2020 = 100 for regional comparison), enabling direct visual comparison of relative movements on a unified scale.

4.
Key Finding
Hallazgo Principal

The multi-market analysis reveals that the COVID-19 shock did not affect all grain markets equally — a finding that would be invisible in aggregate national price statistics.

Integrated Findings
Hallazgos Integrados
1. Currency shock: USD/COP depreciated 43.7% from April 2018 low (COP 2,765) to April 2020 peak (COP 3,974). The sharpest movement was +14.4% MoM in March 2020.

2. Oil shock: Brent crude collapsed 76.0% from October 2018 peak ($81.42/bbl) to April 2020 trough ($19.53/bbl). The sharpest declines were −42.7% MoM (March) and −38.5% MoM (April).

3. Correlation: USD/COP vs. Brent r = −0.82, confirming that peso depreciation tracked the oil price collapse as USD inflows from petroleum exports declined.

4. Fríjol volatility: CV at Corabastos rose from 6.02% (Mar–Aug 2019) to 11.34% (Mar–Aug 2020) — an 88.4% increase. The sharpest monthly surge was +28.3% MoM in April 2020.

5. Regional divergence: Bogotá-Ibagué fríjol spread widened from 8.2% to 14.7% during April–June 2020, indicating temporary market segmentation during peak lockdown.

6. Product heterogeneity: Rice showed minimal volatility increase (CV change ~2 pp) and stable regional spreads (6–8%), reflecting its more formalized supply chain.

5.
What I Learned
Lo que Aprendí

The capstone taught me how to integrate multiple analytical threads into a coherent narrative. Each research brief (fríjol volatility, exchange rate effects, regional comparison) addresses one dimension; the capstone shows that these dimensions interact — volatility, exchange rate transmission, and geographic segmentation are not independent phenomena but facets of the same market disruption.

Building a correlation matrix across heterogeneous series — domestic food prices (COP/kg), exchange rates (COP/USD), and commodity prices (USD/bbl) — introduced the challenge of unit alignment and the power of normalized metrics (base-100 indexing) for cross-series comparison.

The experience of writing multiple regression in LINEST — manually constructing the design matrix, interpreting coefficient arrays, and diagnosing collinearity — built the econometric intuition that makes Python's statsmodels output immediately readable in 2024–2025 projects.

6.
Limitation
Limitación
Scope & Method Limitation
Alcance y Método Limitación
The analysis covers three grain products and two cities. A comprehensive study would include perishable products (fruits, vegetables), additional regional markets, and weekly rather than monthly frequency. The OLS regression uses contemporaneous variables without testing lag structures or controlling for heteroscedasticity/autocorrelation — diagnostics addressed in the 2022 Capstone with formal time-series methods. High collinearity between USD/COP and Brent (r = −0.82) inflates coefficient standard errors and prevents reliable isolation of individual variable effects.
7.
Deliverable
Entregable
